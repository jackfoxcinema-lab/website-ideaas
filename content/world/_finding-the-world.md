## 1. Where does each song happen?

Not what it's *about* — where. A room, a car, a road, outside at night.
Vague is fine. "Somewhere cold" is an answer.

- Song 1:
- Song 2:
- Song 3:
- Song 4 (the unreleased one):

If two of them happen in the same place, that place is your first entry.
If all four happen somewhere different, the thing connecting them isn't a
place — skip to question 3.

## 2. What turns up in more than one song?

An object. A word you used twice without noticing. A time of day. Weather.
A colour. A piece of furniture.

The ones you *didn't plan* are the valuable ones — those are the things your
head keeps going back to, which is what a world actually is.

## 3. Who is "you"?

Most songs have a *you* in them. Across your four:

- Same person every time?
- Different people?
- Sometimes yourself?

You don't have to name them. "The one who isn't there" is a figure. So is
"whoever left".

## 4. What's the weather?

Worlds have weather, and it's usually one kind. Is this place cold? Wet?
Too bright? Indoors? Is it the same season in every song or does it move?

## 5. What is song four doing that the first three aren't?

It's finished and it hasn't gone out yet, which means you already know
something about it the audience doesn't.

- Is it more of the same, or a turn?
- Does it answer one of the others?
- Is it the newest thing you've written, or the oldest?

If it's a turn, that turn is an **event** — the hinge the others sit either
side of. That's often the strongest entry in a world.

## 6. What are you actually saying in the sit-down video?

You're editing it now, so you've already decided what's worth explaining.
Whatever you say in it is the honest version of the premise — better than
anything written for a website.

Write down the one sentence you keep coming back to in that video.
That sentence probably belongs at the top of /world.

## Then

Three answers is enough to start. A place, a recurring object, and one
figure gets you three entries, and three entries is a world — people fill
in the rest themselves. That's the whole trick.

Everything here can be wrong later. When a new song contradicts an entry,
rewrite the entry. Nothing on the site is a promise.
