It's the length of a finger and the colour of an old tap. Slightly bent, the
way a key gets bent when it's been used as a screwdriver.

It does not fit the glasshouse. It does not fit the house. It has hung on the
hook by the glasshouse door for as long as the glasshouse has been in the
songs, and every so often somebody picks it up and puts it back.

## The rule

I'm not deciding what it opens. The moment it opens something it stops being
the thing in the songs and starts being a plot point.
