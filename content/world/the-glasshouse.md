Four walls of glass and a roof that leaks in one corner, which is the corner
everything grows best in. Nobody built it for the plants that are in it now.

It faces the wrong way — east, so it gets the cold half of the morning and
none of the afternoon. Whoever put it there was thinking about the view from
the house, not about what would have to live inside it.

## What's in it

Tomatoes that never ripened and were left standing. A wooden bench with one
leg shimmed by a paperback. Sixty-odd terracotta pots stacked inside each
other in a tower that has not fallen over yet.

> and I kept the door propped with a brick\
> so the heat could get out
> and so could I

## What it's for

It's where things are kept while a decision gets made about them. That is
true of the plants and it is true of everything else.
