You never meet them. You arrive and the floor is wet and the door is propped
and the watering can is back on its hook with the handle facing out, which is
not how you left it.

I've tried twice to write a song where they turn round and it doesn't work.
The second you give them a face the whole place shrinks to the size of a
story about two people.

## What we know

- They are tidier than the narrator.
- They have been there longer.
- They do not want to be thanked, and one song is about finding that out the
  hard way.
