Frost forms on the inside of glass when the air indoors is wetter than the
air outside and nothing is moving. That winter it did it for eleven weeks and
you couldn't see out of the glasshouse at all, only a kind of white light
coming through.

## Why it matters

It's the hinge. Songs set before it are about keeping things alive; songs set
after it are about what you do with the space once you've stopped.

Not every song says which side it's on. That's on purpose — some of them are
still deciding.
