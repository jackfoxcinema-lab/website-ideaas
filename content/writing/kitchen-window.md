I recorded this one on a phone, standing up, at about two in the morning. There is a fridge running the whole time. You can hear it under the second line, a low hum sitting just under the guitar.

The obvious move is to re-record it properly. I've done that before and the song always comes back a little worse — tidier, slower, more careful. Something about knowing the take counts makes me sand the edges off.

## What the take has that a proper one wouldn't

The timing is wrong in two places and I like both of them. The word "window" lands late because I was still deciding whether to sing it. That hesitation is the most honest thing on the recording.

> and the light came through the kitchen window\
> like it had somewhere to be

If I cut a clean version, that line arrives on the beat and means nothing.

## What happens next

I'll probably build something around it eventually — bass, a pad, some room noise to hide the fridge behind. But the vocal stays. Whatever gets built has to fit around a take that was never meant to be kept.

That's mostly what this site is for: keeping the version that was never meant to be kept.
