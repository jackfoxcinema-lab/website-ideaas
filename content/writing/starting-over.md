There is a folder on my laptop called `ideas` and it has a hundred and forty things in it. Most are under a minute. A few are four bars looped for six minutes while I hum over the top trying to find a melody that never arrives.

For a long time the plan was to go back through it and finish things. That plan is about three years old now and the folder has only got bigger.

## The new plan

Post them unfinished.

Not as a statement about process — just because a fragment that somebody hears is more alive than a fragment nobody does. A loop I put up somewhere gets listened to, occasionally gets a reply, and sometimes that reply tells me what the song was about, which I did not know.

## What counts as postable

I settled on three rules:

- It has to have one idea in it I'd defend.
- It has to be listenable start to finish, even if that's twenty seconds.
- It does not have to be good.

The third one is doing most of the work.

## What I'm keeping to myself

Not everything. Some of the folder is genuinely just noise, and some of it is close enough to finished that posting it now would cost me the finished version later.

But that's maybe fifteen files out of a hundred and forty. The rest were only ever waiting for a reason.
